Privacy Policy — Ontime

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

Ontime is a routine reminder app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app does and does not collect.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect any personal information
  • We do not transmit any data to external servers
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools
  • We do not share data with third parties

Data Stored on Your Device

Ontime stores your routine data (title, time, days) locally on your device only using device storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions Used

  • Notifications — to remind you of your routines at scheduled times
  • Exact Alarm — to trigger reminders at precise times
